# Source notes

## Public source

- IdeaBrowser, “Student loan plan picker for borrowers kicked off SAVE”: https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/student-loan-plan-picker-for-borrowers-kicked-off-save-7306dde7
- Dated public email edition: https://www.ideabrowser.com/emails/2026-09-29

The source describes a timely information business for federal student-loan borrowers facing changed payments after disruption to the SAVE plan. Its core thesis is that regulation, increased costs, and conflicting information create demand for one trusted resource. It cites roughly 7 million affected borrowers and more than 40,000 monthly searches, and proposes content, a loan-and-income quiz, a free teaser playbook, and an optional DIY service.

## Retrieval notes

The public idea URL returned a Vercel security checkpoint to a text fetch and redirected the browser fallback to login. No subscriber-only page was copied or published. The prototype therefore uses only the public URL and the concise public-email summary above; scorecards, pricing ladders, keyword tables, revenue projections, and difficulty scores were not available and are not invented.

## Product interpretation

RepayRoute narrows the broad “best resource” thesis into one anxious moment: a borrower receives a payment-change notice and needs to prepare for a servicer conversation. The demo converts a small set of financial inputs into a deterministic 10-year payment estimate, an explicitly illustrative income-linked comparison, a pressure flag, and a documentation checklist. It does not recommend or enroll the user in a repayment plan.

## Research boundaries

- Fictional borrower records only.
- No raw newsletter body, private tracking URLs, subscriber metadata, or unsubscribe information.
- Federal policy is volatile. Calculations are educational estimates, not official plan quotes or financial/legal advice.
- The production research plan starts with borrower observation and certified nonprofit counselor review before adding automation.
